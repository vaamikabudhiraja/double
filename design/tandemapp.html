The insight

The calendar creates the loneliness. We meet it there.

Every social app fights an empty room. Kindred doesn't, because the city seeds it for us. Each September and February, thousands land in Dublin at once — new students, new arrivals for work — all not knowing anyone, all in the same short window of openness.

That's the rarest thing in social products: people who are lonely, motivated and available at the same moment. We show up exactly when it peaks.

How it fits together

Three engines, one funnel

Arrive not knowing anyone → find your first real thing to do → settle into a group you'll keep seeing.

Acquisition

Cohort groups

Uni intakes and newcomers. Timed floods of people who are all new together.

Activation

Event rooms

A concrete first thing to do. Someone's already going to that gig — don't go alone.

Retention

Interest groups

Hikes, wine, board games, doubles. Where people go once they're no longer new.

Open app, per-group doorkeepers

You don't verify to get in. You verify to join specific groups.

Anyone in Dublin can join Kindred and use the gigs, interest groups and newcomer stuff freely. Verification only appears the moment you tap into a group that needs it — you prove you're a UCD student to enter the UCD group, and nowhere else. Same check, but it feels earned instead of interrogating.

So a badge is something you carry per group, and a profile can hold several — each proving the one thing that group cares about:

✓ UCD student ✓ Trinity student ✓ New to Dublin ✓ ID verified (safety) ✓ open group — no check

What to build first

Don't build all of it. Build the wedge.

1

One city, one scene

Indie/rock gigs at the mid-size rooms. Ticketmaster search only. Solo-seeks-companion plus a small pre-gig room. Nothing else yet.

2

Seed it by hand

Go to those gigs, get people on, and time the first real push to the September intake so the app is busy from day one.

3

Switch on cohorts

Open the UCD / Trinity / TU Dublin and New-to-Dublin groups with per-group verification. Acquisition engine on.

4

Layer the groups

Once there's density and trust, open recurring interest groups, doubles and couples on a base that already works.

Plumbing: Ticketmaster's Discovery API is public and dominant in Ireland — your spine. Eventbrite killed public event search in 2019, so let users paste those links manually instead.

The name

tandem

Two people moving together — nobody pedalling alone. It carries the doubles-and-pairs idea in one word, and scales past Dublin if you ever want it to.
